# pCloud Password Manager Hit by Cyber Attack Involving Email Address Exposure and Login Attempts

**Category:** Cybersecurity

**Original source:** https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/pcloud-password-manager-hit-by-cyber.html

# Security Alert: pCloud Pass Users Targeted in Credential Stuffing Campaign

Users of the cross-platform password manager pCloud Pass are being urged to review their account security following a confirmed cyber attack. The incident, which came to light after customers reported suspicious activity on their accounts, has prompted the Swiss-based company to issue a formal response.

### What Happened?
The security breach was identified after multiple users took to platforms like Reddit to report that unknown devices were being connected to their pCloud Pass accounts without authorization. According to the company, these reports were the result of a targeted cyber attack.

The pCloud team stated that they have successfully contained the incident. However, the company acknowledged that the attackers managed to gain access to a subset of user data. Specifically, a limited number of pCloud Pass user email addresses were exposed during the breach. These addresses were subsequently used by attackers to attempt unauthorized logins into the service.

### Why This Matters
For users of password managers, this development is significant. Password managers are designed to be the "digital vault" for a user's most sensitive credentials. When a service that stores these credentials experiences a breach, it creates a high-stakes scenario where attackers may attempt to gain access to the master accounts that protect all other stored passwords.

Even though the company reports that the situation is contained, the exposure of email addresses provides malicious actors with the necessary identifiers to launch further phishing campaigns or credential-stuffing attacks.

### Recommended Steps for Users
While pCloud has confirmed the containment of the attack, users should remain vigilant. If you are a pCloud Pass user, consider the following actions:

*   **Review Account Activity:** Check your account settings for any unrecognized devices and remove them immediately.
*   **Monitor for Phishing:** Be extra cautious of emails claiming to be from pCloud, especially those asking you to reset your password or provide sensitive information.
*   **Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** If you have not already, ensure that MFA is active on your account to provide an extra layer of defense against unauthorized login attempts.

**Verification Needed:** At this time, the full scope of the breach—including the exact number of affected users and whether any master passwords or encrypted data were compromised—remains unclear. Users should monitor official communication channels from pCloud for further updates and specific instructions on whether a password reset is required for their accounts.

### Conclusion
The incident serves as a stark reminder that even security-focused tools are not immune to targeted attacks. While pCloud has taken steps to mitigate the threat, users should proactively secure their accounts to prevent further unauthorized access.
