Artificial Intelligence

How Generative AI Is Changing Creativity

Generative Artificial Intelligence is a branch of AI that can produce new content such as text, images, audio, video and computer code. It learns patterns from large datasets and uses those patterns to generate responses to user instructions.

Generative AI is being used in education, content creation, software development, research and business. It can help people brainstorm ideas, explain complex subjects and complete repetitive tasks. However, generated information should always be reviewed because AI systems can sometimes produce inaccurate or misleading results.

Machine Learning

Machine Learning Explained Simply

Machine Learning is a field of Artificial Intelligence that allows computers to learn patterns from data instead of relying only on manually written rules. A model is trained using examples and then uses what it has learned to make predictions on new data.

Machine Learning is used in recommendation systems, spam detection, fraud identification, image classification and medical research. The quality of its predictions depends on factors such as training data, model design and evaluation.

Cybersecurity

Phishing Attacks: How to Recognise Them

Phishing is a cyberattack in which criminals impersonate trusted organisations or individuals to trick people into sharing passwords, financial details or other sensitive information.

These attacks may arrive through emails, text messages, social media or fake websites. Be cautious of unexpected messages, urgent requests, suspicious links and attachments. Always verify the sender through an official channel before sharing personal information.

Emerging Technology

The Internet of Things: A Connected World

The Internet of Things (IoT) refers to physical devices connected to the internet that can collect, exchange and sometimes act on data. Examples include smart watches, connected cameras, industrial sensors and smart home appliances.

IoT technology can improve convenience, monitoring and automation. However, poorly secured devices may expose networks to cyber threats. Strong passwords, software updates and secure network configurations are important for protecting connected devices.
